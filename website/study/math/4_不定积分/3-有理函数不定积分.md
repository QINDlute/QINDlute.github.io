# 有理函数不定积分
$\begin{align*}R(x) = \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}. where P(x)、Q(x) 为多项式.\end{align*}$

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\deg P(x) \ge \deg Q(x). R(x)-假分式;$
<br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\deg P(x) < \deg Q(x). R(x)-真分式.$

对于 $\int R(x) \mathrm{d}x:$  
$1^0.~If~R(x)~为假分式,则~R(x) = 多项式 + 真分式.$
::: details: 多项式除法
$当~R(x)~为假分式,则可使用多项式除法将~R(x)~分解为多项式 + 真分式.$  
$下面介绍多项式除法$  
$\begin{align*}例如:\frac{x^4 + 5x^3 - x + 4}{x^2 - x - 2}\end{align*}$  
$\begin{array}{lr} 
& x^2+\;\;6x+8\; \\[-3pt]
x^2-x-2  \!\!\!\!\!\! & \enclose{longdiv}{x^4+5x^3+0x^2-\quad x+4} \\[-3pt]
& \underline{x^4-\;\;x^3-2x^2\quad\quad\quad\quad\;\;\;} \\[-3pt]
& 6x^3 + 2x^2 - \quad x\quad\quad \\[-3pt]
& \underline{6x^3 - 6x^2 - 12x\quad\quad} \\[-3pt]
& 8x^2 + 11x \;+ 4 \\[-3pt]
& \underline{8x^2 - \;8x - 16} \\[-3pt]
& 19x + 20 \\
\end{array}$  
$\begin{align*}\Rightarrow \frac{x^4 + 5x^3 - x + 4}{x^2 - x - 2} = x^2+6x+8 + \frac{19x+20}{x^2-x-2}\end{align*}$

:::

$2^0.~If~R(x)~为真分式:R(x)=\frac{分子不变}{分母因式分解} = 部分和$  
① $\begin{align*}R(x) = \frac{19x+20}{x^2-x-2} = \frac{19x+20}{(x+1)(x-2)} = \frac{A}{x+1} + \frac{B}{x-2}\end{align*}$  
$由A(x-2)+B(x+1) = 19x + 20 \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{split} A+B &= 19 \\ -2A+B &= 20 \end{split} \right.$  
② $\begin{align*}R(x) = \frac{x^2-4x+11}{(x-1)^3(2x+1)} = \frac{A}{x-1} + \frac{B}{(x-1)^2} + \frac{C}{(x-1)^3} + \frac{D}{2x+1}\end{align*}$  
③ $\begin{align*}R(x) = \frac{2x^3 + 1}{x^2(x^2-x+1)} = \frac{A}{x} + \frac{B}{x^2} + \frac{Cx + D}{x^2-x+1}\end{align*}$  

例1.  
(1)  
$\begin{align*}
\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2-x-6} &= \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x-3)(x+2)} = \frac{1}{5} \int \Big(\frac{1}{x-3} - \frac{1}{x+2} \Big) \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac{1}{5} \ln \Big|\frac{x-3}{x+2}\Big| + C.
\end{align*}$  
(2)  
$\begin{align*}
\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2+x+1} &= \int \frac{\mathrm{d}(x+\frac{1}{2})}{(\frac{\sqrt 3}{2})^2 + (x+\frac{1}{2})^2} = \frac{1}{\frac{\sqrt 3}{2}} \arctan \Big(\frac{x + \frac{1}{2}}{\frac{\sqrt 3}{2}} \Big) + C \\
&= \frac{2 \sqrt 3}{3} \arctan (\frac{2 \sqrt 3}{3}x + \frac{\sqrt 3}{3}) + C.
\end{align*}$  

例2.  
(1)$\begin{align*}\int \frac{5x + 1}{x^2 - x - 2} \mathrm{d}x\end{align*}$  
$\begin{array}{l}
\begin{aligned}
解:\frac{5x + 1}{x^2-x-2} = \frac{5x + 1}{(x+1)(x-2)} = \frac{A}{x-2} + \frac{B}{x+1}.
\end{aligned} \\
由~A(x+1) + B(x-2) = 5x + 1 \\
\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{split} A+B &= 5 \\ A-2B &= 1 \end{split} \right. \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{split} A &= \frac{11}{3} \\ B &= \frac{4}{3} \end{split} \right. \\
\begin{aligned}原式 = \frac{11}{3}\ln |x-2| + \frac{4}{3}\ln |x+1| + C.\end{aligned}
\end{array}$  
(2)  
$\begin{align*}
\int \frac{x-1}{x^2+x+1} \mathrm{d}x &= \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{(2x + 1) - 3}{x^2 + x + 1} \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \frac{1}{2} \int \frac{\mathrm{d}(x^2 + x + 1)}{x^2 + x + 1} - \frac{3}{2}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}(x+\frac{1}{2})}{(\frac{\sqrt 3}{2})^2 + (x+\frac{1}{2})^2} \\
&= \frac{1}{2}\ln (x^2 + x + 1) - \sqrt 3 \arctan (\frac{2 \sqrt 3}{3}x + \frac{\sqrt 3}{3}) + C.
\end{align*}$  

例3. $\begin{align*}\int \frac{2x^2 + 3}{(x - 1)(x^2 + 1)} \mathrm{d}x\end{align*}$  
$\begin{array}{l}
\begin{aligned}
解:\frac{x^2+3}{(x-1)(x^2+1)} = \frac{A}{x-1} + \frac{Bx + C}{x^2+1}.
\end{aligned} \\
由~A(x^2+1) + (Bx+C)(x-1) = x^2 + 3 \\
\Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{split} A+B &= 1 \\ C-B &= 0 \\ A-C &= 3 \end{split} \right. \Rightarrow \left\{ \begin{split} A &= 2 \\ B &= -1 \\ C &= -1 \end{split} \right. \\
\begin{aligned}
原式 &= 2\ln |x-1| - \frac{1}{2}(\int \frac{2x}{x^2+1}\mathrm{d}x + \int \frac{2}{x^2+1}\mathrm{d}x) \\
&= 2\ln |x-1| - \frac{1}{2}(\int \frac{\mathrm{d}(x^2+1)}{x^2+1} + 2\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2+1}) \\
&= 2\ln |x-1| - \frac{1}{2}\ln (x^2+1) - \arctan x + C.
\end{aligned}
\end{array}$  

例4. $\begin{align*}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x(x^4 + 2)}\end{align*}$  
<Badge type="tip">法一</Badge>

$\begin{align*}
\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x(x^4 + 2)} &= \int \frac{x^3\mathrm{d}x}{x^4(x^4 + 2)} = \frac{1}{4}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x^4}{x^4(x^4 + 2)} \\
&\xlongequal{\text { 令 } t=x^4} \frac{1}{4}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}t}{t(t+2)} = \frac{1}{8}\int (\frac{1}{t} - \frac{1}{t+2}) \mathrm{d}t \\
&= \frac{1}{8} \ln \Big|\frac{t}{t+2}\Big| + C \\
&= \frac{1}{8} \ln \Big|\frac{x^4}{x^4+2}\Big| + C.
\end{align*}$

<Badge type="tip">法二</Badge>

$\begin{array}{l}
\begin{aligned}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x(x^4 + 2)} \xlongequal{\text { 令 } x=\sqrt{t}} \int \frac{\mathrm{d}(\sqrt{t})}{\sqrt{t}(t^2+2)} = \frac{1}{2}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}t}{t(t^2+2)}\end{aligned} \\
\begin{aligned}\because \frac{1}{t(t^2+2)} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{t} + \frac{-\frac{1}{2}t}{t^2+2}\end{aligned} \\
\begin{aligned}
\therefore \frac{1}{2}\int \frac{\mathrm{d}t}{t(t^2+2)} &= \frac{1}{4}\int(\frac{1}{t} - \frac{t}{t^2+2})\mathrm{d}t = \frac{1}{4}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}t}{t} - \frac{1}{4}\int\frac{t}{t^2+2}\mathrm{d}t \\
&= \frac{1}{4}\ln|t| - \frac{1}{8}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}(t^2+2)}{t^2+2} = \frac{1}{8}\ln t^2 - \frac{1}{8}\ln (t^2+2) \\
&= \frac{1}{8}\ln \frac{t^2}{t^2+2} + C \\
&= \frac{1}{8}\ln \frac{x^4}{x^4+2} + C.
\end{aligned}
\end{array}$  

例5.  
(1)  
$\begin{align*}
\int \frac{x^2+1}{x^4+1} \mathrm{d}x &= \int \frac{1+\frac{1}{x^2}}{x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}} \mathrm{d}x = \int \frac{\mathrm{d}(x-\frac{1}{x})}{(\sqrt{2})^2 + (x-\frac{1}{x})^2} \\
&= \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \arctan \Big(\frac{x-\frac{1}{x}}{\sqrt{2}}\Big) + C.
\end{align*}$  

(2)  
$\begin{align*}
\int \frac{x^2-1}{x^4+1} \mathrm{d}x &= \int \frac{1-\frac{1}{x^2}}{x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}} \mathrm{d}x = \int \frac{\mathrm{d}(x+\frac{1}{x})}{(x+\frac{1}{x})^2 - (\sqrt{2})^2 } \\
&= \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}} \ln \Big|\frac{x+\frac{1}{x}-\sqrt{2}}{x+\frac{1}{x}+\sqrt{2}}\Big| + C.
\end{align*}$

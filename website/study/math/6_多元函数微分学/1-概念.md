# 基本概念
## 1.极限

[一元极限](/study/math/1_极限与连续/1-定义#极限定义)：设函数 $f(x)$ 在 $x=x_0$ 的某个去心领域内有定义.  
<Badge type="warning">If</Badge>$\forall ~\varepsilon > 0,\exists ~\delta > 0,$ 当 $0 < |x - x_0| < \delta$ 时，恒有:  
$|f(x) - A| < \varepsilon.$  
记作 $\lim\limits_{x \to x_0} f(x) = A.$

<div class="custom-block red">

$\displaystyle\lim_{x \to x_0} f(x) ~\exists~ \Longleftrightarrow f(x_0-0)、f(x_0+0) ~\exists~ 且相等$
</div>

二元极限：$f(x,y)$ 在 $M_0(x_0,y_0)$ 的某个去心领域内有定义.  
<Badge type="warning">If</Badge>$\forall ~\varepsilon > 0, \exists ~\delta > 0,$ 当 $0 < \sqrt{(x-x_0)^2+(y-y_0)^2} < \delta$ 时，恒有:

$|f(x,y) - A| < \varepsilon.$  
记作 $\lim\limits_{(x,y) \to (x_0,y_0)} f(x,y) = A$ 或 $\lim\limits_{\begin{aligned}x \to x_0 \\ y \to y_0\end{aligned}} f(x,y) = A.${.overflowx}


## 2.连续

[一元连续](/study/math/1_极限与连续/1-定义#连续定义)：设函数 $f(x)$ 在 $x=x_0$ 的某个去心领域内有定义,且 $\lim\limits_{x \to x_0} f(x) = f(x_0)$  
则称函数 $f(x)$ 在 $x=x_0$ 处连续.

<div class="custom-block red">

$\displaystyle f(x) ~在~ x=x_0 ~连续 \Longleftrightarrow f(x_0-0)=f(x_0+0)=f(x_0)$
</div>

二元连续：设函数 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(x_0,y_0)$ 的某领域内有定义  
且 $\lim\limits_{(x,y) \to (x_0,y_0)} f(x,y) = f(x_0,y_0)$ 或  
$\lim\limits_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)} \Delta z = \lim\limits_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)} [f(x_0+\Delta x,y_0+\Delta y)-f(x_0,y_0)] = 0$  
则称 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(x_0,y_0)$ 处连续.


## 3.偏导数

一元导数：$y=f(x)$ 在 $x=a$ 领域内有定义
<Badge type="warning">If</Badge>$\displaystyle\lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} ~\exists$ 或 $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)-f(a)}{x-a} ~\exists$

<div class="custom-block red">

① $f(x) 在 x=a 可导 \begin{array}{c}\Longrightarrow \\[-4pt] \centernot{\Longleftarrow}\end{array} f'(x) 在 x=a 连续$  
② $f'(a) ~\exists~ \Longleftrightarrow f_-(a),f_+(a) ~\exists~ 且相等$  
③ $f(x) ~在~ x=a ~可导 \Longleftrightarrow f(x) ~在~ x=a ~可微$
</div>

二元偏导数：$z=f(x,y)$ 在 $P_0(x_0,y_0)\in D$ 领域内有定义  
$\Delta z_x = f(x_0+\Delta x,y_0) - f(x_0,y_0)$  
$\Delta z_y = f(x_0,y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0)$  
$\Delta z = f(x_0+\Delta x, y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0)$  
<Badge type="warning">If</Badge>$\displaystyle\lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\Delta z_x}{\Delta x} ~\exists$ 或 $\displaystyle\lim_{x \to x_0} \frac{f(x,y_0)-f(x_0,y_0)}{x-x_0} ~\exists$

称 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(x_0,y_0)$ 处关于 $x$ 可偏导，极限值为对 $x$ 的偏导数.  
记作 $f_x(x_0,y_0)$ 或 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\Big|_{(x_0,y_0)}$

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge>$\displaystyle\lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{\Delta z_y}{\Delta y} ~\exists$ 或 $\displaystyle\lim_{y \to y_0} \frac{f(x_0,y)-f(x_0,y_0)}{y-y_0} ~\exists$

称 $f(x,y)$ 在 $(x_0,y_0)$ 处关于 $y$ 可偏导，极限值为对 $y$ 的偏导数.  
记作 $f_y(x_0,y_0)$ 或 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\Big|_{(x_0,y_0)}$

---
称 $\displaystyle\lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x+\Delta x,y)-f(x,y)}{\Delta x} \triangleq f_x(x,y)$ 为 $f(x,y)$ 关于 $x$ 的偏导函数  
设 $z=f(x,y)$ 的偏导函数 $f_x(x,y),f_y(x,y)$ 仍可偏导  
$\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} = f_{xx}(x,y),\;\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} = f_{yy}(x,y);$  
$\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x \partial y} = \frac{\partial}{\partial y}\Bigg(\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\Bigg) \triangleq f_{xy}(x,y);$  
$\displaystyle\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y \partial x} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\Bigg(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\Bigg) \triangleq f_{yx}(x,y).$

::: faq-math \begin{array}{l}例1.讨论函数~f(x,y)=\left\{\begin{split}&\frac{xy}{x^2+y^2}&,(x,y)\ne(0,0), \\ &0 &,(x,y)=(0,0)\end{split}\right.~在(0,0)处的连续性 \\ 和偏导的存在性.\end{array}#

$\begin{array}{l}
\displaystyle 若~\lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} f(x,y) = f(0,0) ~则~ f(x,y) ~在~ (0,0) ~处连续 \\
\displaystyle 当~y=kx ~时,\lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} f(x,y) = \lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} \frac{k}{1+k^2}\ne f(0,0)=0~(k\ne 0) \\
\therefore f(x,y) ~在~ (0,0) ~处不连续 \\
\displaystyle f_x(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(\Delta x,0)-f(0,0)}{\Delta x} = 0,f_y(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(0,\Delta y)-f(0,0)}{\Delta y} = 0 \\
f_x(0,0) = f_y(0,0) = 0 \\
\therefore f(x,y)~在~ (0,0) ~处偏导数存在
\end{array}$

:::

::: faq-math \begin{array}{l}例2.讨论函数~f(x,y)=\left\{\begin{split}&\frac{x^3+y^3}{x^2+y^2}&,(x,y)\ne(0,0), \\ &0 &,(x,y)=(0,0)\end{split}\right.~在(0,0)处的连续性 \\ 和偏导的存在性.\end{array}#

$\begin{array}{l}
\displaystyle \because 0 \le \Big|\frac{x^3+y^3}{x^2+y^2}\Big| \le \Big|\frac{x^3}{x^2+y^2}\Big|+\Big|\frac{y^3}{x^2+y^2}\Big| = \Big|\frac{x^3}{x^2}\Big|+\Big|\frac{y^3}{y^2}\Big| = |x|+|y| \\
\displaystyle \therefore 0 \le \lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} |f(x,y)| \le \lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} |x|+|y| = 0 \\
\displaystyle \therefore \lim_{(x,y) \to (0,0)} |f(x,y)| = f(0,0) = 0 \Rightarrow f(x,y) ~在~ (0,0) ~处连续 \quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad\quad \\
\displaystyle f_x(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(\Delta x,0)-f(0,0)}{\Delta x} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta x} = 1 \\
\displaystyle f_y(0,0) = \lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(0,\Delta y)-f(0,0)}{\Delta y} = \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta y} = 1 \\
\therefore f(x,y)~在~ (0,0) ~处偏导数存在
\end{array}$

:::


## 4.可（全）微

一元可微：$y=f(x)$ 在 $x=a$ 领域内有定义  
$\Delta y = f(a+\Delta x) - f(a)(=f(x)-f(a))$  
<Badge type="warning">If</Badge>$\Delta y = A\Delta x + o(\Delta x)$

称 $f(x)$ 在 $x=a$ 处可微:  
$\begin{array}{l}A\Delta x \triangleq \mathrm dy\big|_{x=a} \\ \quad\parallel \\ A\mathrm dx \end{array}${.overflowx}

<div class="custom-block purple">

① $f(x) ~在~ x=a ~可微 \Longleftrightarrow f(x) ~在~ x=a ~可微$  
② $If~ \Delta y = A\Delta x + o(\Delta x), 则 A = f'(a)$  
③ $If~ y = f(x) 可导, 则 \mathrm dy = \mathrm df(x) = f'(x)\mathrm dx.$
</div>

二元可微：$z=f(x,y)$ 在 $(x_0,y_0)$ 领域内有定义  
$\Delta z = f(x_0+\Delta x,y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0)~或$  
$\Delta z = f(x,y)-f(x_0,y_0) ~(\Delta x = x-x_0,\Delta y = y-y_0)$  
$If~\Delta z = A\Delta x + B\Delta y + o\big(\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2}\big)$  
其中 $A,B$ 仅依赖于 $(x,y)$ 而与 $\Delta x,\Delta y$ 无关，则称 $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(x,y)$ 可(全)微  
其中 $A\Delta x + B\Delta y$ 称为 $f(x,y)$ 在点 $(x,y)$ 的全微分，记作 $\mathrm dz:$  
$\begin{array}{l}A\Delta x + B\Delta y \triangleq \mathrm dz\big|_{(x_0,y_0)} \\ \quad\quad\parallel \\ A\mathrm dx + B\mathrm dy\end{array}${.overflowx}

<div class="custom-block red">

①  
$\begin{align*}&当~ \Delta z = A\Delta x + B\Delta y + o\big(\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2}\big) \\
&\Rightarrow A = f_x(x_0,y_0),~B = f_y(x_0,y_0)  \\
&则称~f(x,y)~在~(x_0,y_0)~可微.
\end{align*}$  
$可微 \begin{array}{c}\Longrightarrow \\[-4pt] \centernot{\Longleftarrow}\end{array} 可偏导$  
② $当~ z=f(x,y) ~处处可微,则:可微 \begin{array}{c}\Longrightarrow \\[-4pt] \centernot{\Longleftarrow}\end{array} 连续$  
$\displaystyle 且~\mathrm dz = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\mathrm dx + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\mathrm dy.$  
③ $一阶偏导连续 \begin{array}{c}\Longrightarrow \\[-4pt] \centernot{\Longleftarrow}\end{array} 可微$  
</div>

::: note 证明
1) $可微 \Rightarrow 连续$  
$\begin{array}{l}
若~f(x,y)~在~(x_0,y_0)~处可微: \\
\displaystyle \Delta z = f_x(x_0,y_0)\Delta x + f_y(x_0,y_0)\Delta y + o\big(\sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2}\big),~\rho = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2} \\
\displaystyle \Delta z = f(x_0+\Delta x,y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0) \\
\begin{aligned}\lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)} \Delta z &= \lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)} [f_x(x_0+\Delta x,y_0+\Delta y)-f_x(x_0,y_0)] \\
&= \lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)} f_x(x_0,y_0)\Delta x + f_y(x_0,y_0)\Delta y + o(\rho) = 0 \end{aligned} \\
\displaystyle \therefore \lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)} f(x_0+\Delta x,y_0+\Delta y) = f(x_0,y_0).
\end{array}$

2) $一阶偏导连续 \Rightarrow 可微$  
$\begin{array}{l}
\begin{align*}
全增量 ~\Delta z &= f(x_0+\Delta x,y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0) \\
&= f(x_0+\Delta x,y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0+\Delta y) + f(x_0,y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0)
\end{align*} \\
根据~Lagrange~中值定理: \\
\therefore f(x_0+\Delta x,y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0+\Delta y) = f_x(x_0+\theta_1\Delta x,y_0 + \Delta y)\Delta x~(0\le\theta_1\le1) \\
f(x_0,y_0+\Delta y) - f(x_0,y_0) = f_y(x_0,y_0 + \theta_2\Delta y)\Delta y~(0\le\theta_2\le1) \\
\Delta z = f_x(x_0+\theta_1\Delta x,y_0 + \Delta y)\Delta x + f_y(x_0,y_0 + \theta_2\Delta y)\Delta y \\ \\
\displaystyle 根据连续的无穷小形式:\lim_{x\to x_0} f(x) = f(x_0) + \alpha~(x\to x_0,\alpha\to 0) \\
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)} \Delta z &= (f_x(x_0,y_0) + \alpha)\Delta x + (f_y(x_0,y_0) + \beta)\Delta y \\
&= f_x(x_0,y_0)\Delta x + f_y(x_0,y_0)\Delta y + \alpha\Delta x + \beta\Delta y
\end{aligned} \\
此时~\left\{\begin{split}&\Delta x\to 0, \\ &\Delta y\to 0\end{split}\right. \Rightarrow \left\{\begin{split}&\alpha\to 0, \\&\beta\to 0, \\ &\rho\to 0\end{split}\right. \\ \\
\because \rho = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2+(\Delta y)^2} \ge \Delta x 或 \Delta y \\
\displaystyle \therefore 0 \le \lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)}\Big|\frac{\alpha\Delta x + \beta\Delta y}{\rho}\Big| \le \lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)}\Big(\Big|\frac{\Delta x}{\rho}\alpha\Big| + \Big|\frac{\Delta y}{\rho}\beta\Big|\Big) \le \lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)}(|\alpha| + |\beta|) = 0 \\
\displaystyle 由夹逼定理得:\lim_{(\Delta x,\Delta y) \to (0,0)} \Big|\frac{\alpha\Delta x + \beta\Delta y}{\rho}\Big| = 0 \\
\therefore \alpha\Delta x + \beta\Delta y = o(\rho) \\ \\
得~ \Delta z = f_x(x_0,y_0)\Delta x + f_y(x_0,y_0)\Delta y + o(\rho) \\
\Rightarrow f(x,y) ~在~ (x_0,y_0) ~处可微.
\end{array}$

:::

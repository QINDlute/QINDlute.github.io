# 导数推导
导数定义:$f'(x) = \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x}$

1. $f(x) = \sin x$  
$\begin{align*}
f'(x) &= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x} \\
&= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\sin(x+\Delta x)-\sin x}{\Delta x} \\
&= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\sin x \cos \Delta x + \cos x \sin \Delta x - \sin x}{\Delta x} \\
&= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\cos x \sin \Delta x}{\Delta x} \\
&= \cos x
\end{align*}
$

::: details: 证明 x 趋于零时 sinx/x 的极限为 1
$作圆心在原点的单位圆，取圆心角~x \in (0,\frac{\pi}{2}),A~是圆上一点,B(1,0)$  
$过~B~点作~x~轴的垂线与射线~OA~交于~C~点,作外切~Rt \Delta OBC$  
$如图所示,显然圆内三角形<扇形<外切三角形:S_{\Delta OAB} < S_{⌔ OAB} < S_{\Delta OBC}$  
$S_{\Delta OAB} = \frac{1}{2} AO\cdot BO\cdot \sin x = \frac{1}{2} \sin x$  
$S_{⌔ OAB} = \frac{1}{2} x$  
$S_{\Delta OBC} = \frac{1}{2} OB\cdot \tan x = \frac{1}{2} \tan x$  
$\begin{array}{rcl}
\Rightarrow \frac{1}{2} \sin x <& \frac{1}{2} x &< \frac{1}{2} \\
\sin x <& x &< \tan x \\
1 <& \frac{x}{\sin x} &< \frac{1}{\cos x},(x \in (0,\frac{\pi}{2}),\sin x > 0) \\
\cos x <& \frac{\sin x}{x} &< 1
\end{array}$  
$\lim\limits_{x \to 0^{+}} \cos x = \cos 0 = 1$  
$由夹逼准则得到:\lim\limits_{x \to 0^{+}} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1$  
$令~t = -x,则~x \to 0^{-} \Longleftrightarrow t \to 0^{+}$  
$\lim\limits_{x \to 0^{-}} \frac{\sin x}{x} = \lim\limits_{t \to 0^{+}} \frac{\sin -t}{-t} = \lim\limits_{t \to 0^{+}} \frac{\sin t}{t} = 1$  
$\therefore \lim\limits_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1,或描述为~x \to 0~时,\sin x\sim x$

2. $f(x) = \cos x$  
$\begin{align*}
f'(x) &= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x+\Delta x)-f(x)}{\Delta x} \\
&= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\cos(x+\Delta x)-\cos x}{\Delta x} \\
&= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\cos x \cos \Delta x - \sin x \sin \Delta x - \cos x}{\Delta x} \\
&= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{- \sin x \sin \Delta x}{\Delta x} \\
&= - \sin x
\end{align*}
$

3. 其他三角函数  
① $\tan x = \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}$  
$\begin{align*}
(\tan x)' &= \frac{(\sin x)'\cos x - \sin x(\cos x)'}{\cos^2 x} \\
&= \frac{\cos x\cdot \cos x - \sin x(- \sin x)}{\cos^2 x} \\
&= \frac{\cos^2 x + \sin^2 x}{\cos^2 x} \\
&= \sec^2 x
\end{align*}$  
② $\cot x = \frac{\cos x}{\sin x} = \frac{1}{\tan x}$  
$\begin{align*}
(\cot x)' &= - \frac{1}{\tan^2 x}\sec^2 x \\
&= - \frac{\cancel{\cos^2 x}}{\sin^2 x}\cdot \frac{1}{\cancel{\cos^2 x}} \\
&= - \csc^2 x
\end{align*}$  
③ $\sec x = \frac{1}{\cos x}$  
$\begin{align*}
(\sec x)' &= \cancel{-} \frac{1}{\cos^2 x}(\cancel{-} \sin x) \\
&= \frac{1}{\cos x}\cdot \frac{\sin x}{\cos x} \\
&= \sec x \tan x
\end{align*}$  
④ $\csc x = \frac{1}{\sin x}$  
$\begin{align*}
(\csc x)' &= - \frac{1}{\sin^2 x}\cos x \\
&= - \frac{1}{\sin x}\cdot \frac{\cos x}{\sin x} \\
&= - \csc x \cot x
\end{align*}$  

---
整理所有三角函数的导数:

$$\begin{align*}
(\sin x)' &= \cos x \\
(\cos x)' &= - \sin x \\
(\tan x)' &= \sec^2 x \\
(\cot x)' &= - \csc^2 x \\
(\sec x)' &= \sec x \tan x \\
(\csc x)' &= - \csc x \cot x \\
\end{align*}$$

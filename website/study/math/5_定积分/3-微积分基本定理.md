# 微积分基本定理
&emsp;&emsp;(1) 定义:设函数 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[a,b]$ 上可积，则称 $\begin{align*}\Phi(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t,a \le x \le b\end{align*}$ 为<span class="mkh-yellow">变上限积分(积分上限函数)；</span>  
&emsp;&emsp;(2) 性质:若 $f(x)$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上可积，则 $\begin{align*}\Phi(x) = \int_a^x f(t) \mathrm{d}t\end{align*}$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上连续；  
&emsp;&emsp;(3) 定理:  
&emsp;&emsp;<Badge type="danger">① 微积分第一基本定理</Badge>

&emsp;&emsp;若函数 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[a,b]$ 上<span color="blue">连续</span>，则变上限积分 $\begin{align*}\Phi(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t\end{align*}$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上可导，且
$$
\Phi'(x) = \Big(\int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t\Big)' = f(x),a \le x \le b;
$$

&emsp;&emsp;<Badge type="danger">② 微积分第二基本定理</Badge>

&emsp;&emsp;设 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[a,b]$ 上<span color="blue">连续</span>，$F(x)$ 是 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[a,b]$ 上的一个原函数，则
$$
\int_a^b f(x)\mathrm{d}x = F(b) - F(a).
$$

::: whiteboard 证明
<Badge type="note">① 微积分第一基本定理</Badge>

$\begin{array}{l}
\begin{aligned}令~\Phi(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t.\end{aligned} \\
\begin{aligned}
\Phi'(x) &= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\Phi(x + \Delta x) - \Phi(x)}{\Delta x} = \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\int_a^{x+\Delta x} f(t)\mathrm{d}t - \int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t}{\Delta x} \\
&= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\cancel{\int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t}+\int_x^{x+\Delta x} f(t)\mathrm{d}t - \cancel{\int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t}}{\Delta x} \\
&= \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{\int_x^{x+\Delta x} f(t)\mathrm{d}t}{\Delta x}
\end{aligned} \\
\begin{aligned}根据积分中值定理:\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = f(\zeta) (b - a),\zeta \in [a,b] \end{aligned} \\
\begin{aligned}\Rightarrow \int_x^{x+\Delta x} f(t)\mathrm{d}t =f(\zeta) \Delta x,\zeta \in [x,x+\Delta x]~(若\Delta x < 0,则~\zeta \in [x+\Delta x,x])\end{aligned} \\
\therefore \Phi'(x) = \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} f(\zeta) \\
根据夹逼定理，当~\Delta x \to 0~时,\zeta \to x,且~f(x)~连续 \\
\Rightarrow \lim\limits_{\Delta x \to 0} f(\zeta) = \lim\limits_{\zeta \to x} f(\zeta) = f(x) \\
即~\Phi'(x) = f(x).
\end{array}$  

>[!TIP] 变上限积分通用求导公式
> $\begin{align*}
\int_{\varphi_1(x)}^{\varphi_2(x)} f(t)\mathrm{d}t &= \int_a^{\varphi_2(x)}f(t)\mathrm{d}t - \int_a^{\varphi_1(x)}f(t)\mathrm{d}t \\
\Big(\int_{\varphi_1(x)}^{\varphi_2(x)} f(t)\mathrm{d}t\Big)' & = f(\varphi_2(x))\varphi_2'(x) - f(\varphi_1(x))\varphi_1'(x) \\
\Big(\int_a^{\varphi(x)} f(t)\mathrm{d}t\Big)' &= f(\varphi(x))\varphi'(x)
\end{align*}$

---
<Badge type="note">② 微积分第二基本定理</Badge>

$\begin{array}{l}
\begin{aligned}设~\Phi(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t,F(x)~是~f(x)~的一个原函数,F(x) = \Phi(x) + C\end{aligned} \\
F(a) = \Phi(a) + C = 0 + C = C \\
\begin{aligned}\Rightarrow \Phi(x) &= F(x) - F(a) \\
\Phi(b) &= F(b) - F(a)
\end{aligned} \\
\begin{aligned}即\int_a^b f(x)\mathrm{d}x &= F(b) - F(a).\end{aligned}
\end{array}$  

---
<Badge type="danger">Notes:</Badge>

(1) 对于 $\begin{align*}\int_a^x f(t)dt.\end{align*}$  
1. 计算积分过程中，t 是自变量，x 作为积分上限是参变量（或外层自变量），视为常数；
2. 积分求导过程中，x 是自变量，t 作为哑变量在积分计算完成时被 x 替换掉了.  

(2) 对于 $\begin{align*}\int_a^x f(x,t)\mathrm{d}t.\end{align*}$  
&emsp;&emsp;此时 $f(x,t)$ 中的 x 与积分上限中的 x 是同一个变量，在积分过程中，x 被视为常数.

:::

::: note 积分中值定理的加强形式证明
$\begin{array}{l}
\begin{aligned}定义~\Phi(x) = \int_a^x f(t)\mathrm{d}t,x \in [a,b],\Phi'(x) = f(x)\end{aligned} \\
由~Lagrange~中值定理得:\exists~\zeta \in (a,b),使得~\Phi(b) - \Phi(a) = \Phi'(\zeta) (b - a) \\
\begin{aligned}由~\Phi(x)~的定义:\Phi(b) = \int_a^b f(t)\mathrm{d}t,\Phi(a) = \int_a^a f(t)\mathrm{d}t = 0,\Phi'(\zeta) = f(\zeta)\end{aligned} \\
\begin{aligned}\Rightarrow \int_a^b f(t)\mathrm{d}t &= f(\zeta)(b-a) \\
\int_a^b f(x)\mathrm{d}x &= f(\zeta)(b-a)~(\zeta \in (a,b)).
\end{aligned}
\end{array}$

:::

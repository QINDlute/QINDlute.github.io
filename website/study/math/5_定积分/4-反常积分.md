# 反常积分
在定积分的定义一节我们提到了黎曼积分可积的一个充分条件:
> $若~f(x)~在~[a,b]~上有界,且存在有限个第一类间断点,则~f(x)~必可积$  

现在我们定义反常积分:  
① $f(x)$ 积分<span class="mkh">区间无限</span>  
② $f(x)$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上有<span class="mkh">瑕点</span>  

满足任意一点即称为反常积分.

## 积分区间无限
1. $f(x) \in c[a,+\infty)$  
<Badge type="note">def -</Badge> $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = F(b)-F(a)$
<br>
<Badge type="danger">Notes:</Badge><br>

<div class="custom-block red inline">

① $\displaystyle F(b)-F(a) \ne \int_a^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x$  
② $\displaystyle\lim_{b \to +\infty} [F(b)-F(a)] = \int_a^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x$  
</div><br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\lim\limits_{b \to +\infty} [F(b)-F(a)] = A.$ 
<Badge type="green">then</Badge> 称 $\displaystyle\int_a^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 收敛于A，记 $\displaystyle\int_a^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x = A;$
<br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\lim\limits_{b \to +\infty} [F(b)-F(a)]$ 不存在. 
<Badge type="green">then</Badge> 称 $\displaystyle\int_a^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 发散.


2. $f(x) \in c(-\infty,a]$  
<Badge type="note">def -</Badge> $\displaystyle\int_b^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x = F(a)-F(b)$
<br>
<Badge type="danger">Notes:</Badge><br>

<div class="custom-block red inline">

① $\displaystyle F(a)-F(b) \ne \int_{-\infty}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x$  
② $\displaystyle\lim_{b \to -\infty} [F(a)-F(b)] = \int_{-\infty}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x$  
</div><br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\lim\limits_{b \to -\infty} [F(a)-F(b)] = A.$ 
<Badge type="green">then</Badge> 称 $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 收敛于A，记 $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x = A;$
<br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\lim\limits_{b \to -\infty} [F(a)-F(b)]$ 不存在. 
<Badge type="green">then</Badge> 称 $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 发散.


3. $f(x) \in c(-\infty,+\infty]$  

<Badge type="note">def -</Badge> $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x = \int_{-\infty}^a f(x)\mathrm{d}x + \int_a^{+\infty} f(x)\mathrm{d}x$
<br>

::: whiteboard 极限形式判别法
1. $f(x) \in c[a,+\infty)~(a>0)$  
① $\displaystyle\exists~ \alpha > 1:\lim_{x \to +\infty} x^\alpha f(x) ~\exists \Rightarrow \int_a^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~收敛;$  
② $\displaystyle\exists~ \alpha \le 1:\lim_{x \to +\infty} x^\alpha f(x) = k(\ne 0)~或~\infty \Rightarrow \int_a^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~发散.$  

2. $f(x) \in c(-\infty,a]~(a<0)$  
① $\displaystyle\exists~ \alpha > 1:\lim_{x \to -\infty} x^\alpha f(x) ~\exists \Rightarrow \int_{-\infty}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~收敛;$  
② $\displaystyle\exists~ \alpha \le 1:\lim_{x \to -\infty} x^\alpha f(x) = k(\ne 0)~或~\infty \Rightarrow \int_{-\infty}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~发散.$  

3. $f(x) \in c(-\infty,+\infty]$  
<Badge type="green">Conc</Badge> $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~收敛 \Longleftrightarrow \int_{-\infty}^a f(x)\mathrm{d}x ~与~ \int_a^{+\infty} f(x)\mathrm{d}x ~都收敛.$

>: 对于反常积分来说，<span class="mkh-red">敛散性是高于奇偶性的</span>，只有确定敛散性后才能使用奇偶性.

例1. $\displaystyle\int_0^{+\infty} \frac{\sqrt x}{1+x^2} \mathrm{d}x~?$  
$\begin{array}{l}
\displaystyle\because \lim_{x \to +\infty} x^{\frac{3}{2}}\cdot \frac{\sqrt x}{1+x^2} = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x^2}{1+x^2} = 1~且~\alpha = \frac{3}{2} > 1 \\
\displaystyle\therefore \int_0^{+\infty} \frac{\sqrt x}{1+x^2} \mathrm{d}x ~收敛.
\end{array}$

例2. $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \frac{x}{1+x^2} \mathrm{d}x~?$  
$\begin{array}{l}
\displaystyle\because \lim_{x \to +\infty} x^1\cdot \frac{x}{1+x^2} = 1(\ne 0)~且~\alpha = 1 \le 1 \\
\displaystyle\therefore \int_0^{+\infty} \frac{x}{1+x^2} \mathrm{d}x ~发散 \\
\displaystyle\therefore \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \frac{x}{1+x^2} \mathrm{d}x ~发散.
\end{array}$

:::

## 积分区间有限
1. 瑕点  
<Badge type="note">def -</Badge>

若函数 $f(x)$ 在 $x=a$ 的任一领域内都无界，则称点 $a$ 为函数 $f(x)$ 的瑕点.  
此时 $f(a\pm 0)$ 不存在.

> 当且仅当该点是<span class="mkh-red">无穷间断点</span>或无界震荡间断点时为瑕点.

2. $f(x) \in c[a,b]$，且 $a$ 为唯一瑕点
<Badge type="note">def -</Badge> $\displaystyle\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} \int_{a+\varepsilon}^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = F(b)-F(a+\varepsilon)$
<br>
<Badge type="danger">Notes:</Badge><br>

<div class="custom-block red inline">

① $\displaystyle F(b)-F(a+\varepsilon) \ne \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x$  
② $\displaystyle\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} [F(b)-F(a+\varepsilon)] = \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x$  
</div><br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\displaystyle\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} [F(b)-F(a+\varepsilon)] = A.$ 
<Badge type="green">then</Badge> 称 $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 收敛于A，记 $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = A;$
<br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\displaystyle\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} [F(b)-F(a+\varepsilon)]$ 不存在 
<Badge type="green">then</Badge> 称 $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 发散.

3. $f(x) \in c[a,b]$，且 $b$ 为唯一瑕点
<Badge type="note">def -</Badge> $\displaystyle\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} \int_a^{b-\varepsilon} f(x) \mathrm{d}x = F(b-\varepsilon)-F(a)$
<br>
<Badge type="danger">Notes:</Badge><br>

<div class="custom-block red inline">

① $\displaystyle F(b-\varepsilon)-F(a) \ne \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x$  
② $\displaystyle\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} [F(b-\varepsilon)-F(a)] = \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x$  
</div><br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\displaystyle\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} [F(b-\varepsilon)-F(a)] = A.$ 
<Badge type="green">then</Badge> 称 $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 收敛于A，记 $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = A;$
<br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge> $\displaystyle\lim_{\varepsilon \to 0^+} [F(b-\varepsilon)-F(a)]$ 不存在 
<Badge type="green">then</Badge> 称 $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x$ 发散.


::: whiteboard 极限形式判别法
1. $f(x) \in c[a,b],a~为唯一瑕点(无穷间断点)$  
① $\displaystyle\exists~ \alpha < 1:\lim_{x \to a+} (x-a)^\alpha f(x) ~\exists \Rightarrow \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~收敛;$  
② $\displaystyle\exists~ \alpha \ge 1:\lim_{x \to a+} (x-a)^\alpha f(x) = k(\ne 0)~或~\infty \Rightarrow \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~发散.$  
2. $f(x) \in c[a,b],b~为唯一瑕点(无穷间断点)$  
① $\displaystyle\exists~ \alpha < 1:\lim_{x \to b-} (b-x)^\alpha f(x) ~\exists \Rightarrow \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~收敛;$  
② $\displaystyle\exists~ \alpha \ge 1:\lim_{x \to b-} (b-x)^\alpha f(x) = k(\ne 0)~或~\infty \Rightarrow \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~发散.$  
3. $\displaystyle f(x) \in c[a,c)\cup (c,b] ~且~ \lim_{x \to c} f(x) = \infty$  
<Badge type="green">Conc</Badge> $\displaystyle\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x ~收敛 \Longleftrightarrow \int_a^c f(x)\mathrm{d}x ~与~ \int_c^b f(x)\mathrm{d}x ~都收敛.$

例1. $\displaystyle\int_1^2 \frac{1}{x\sqrt{x-1}} \mathrm dx$  
例2. $\displaystyle\int_0^2 \frac{1}{\sqrt{2x-x^2}} \ \mathrm dx$

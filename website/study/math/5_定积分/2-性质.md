# 定积分-性质
## 基本性质
<Badge type="note">1.规定</Badge>

(1) $\begin{align*}\int_a^b f(x)\mathrm{d}x = \int_a^b f(t)\mathrm{d}t\end{align*}$  
(2) $\begin{align*}\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = -\int_b^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x,~特例:\int_a^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x = 0,\int_b^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = 0\end{align*}$
::: warning 注意
对于不定积分:$\int x^2 \mathrm{d}x \ne \int t^2 \mathrm{d}t$  
但对于定积分:$\int_a^b x^2 \mathrm{d}x = \int_a^b t^2 \mathrm{d}t$  
我们可以得出结论:定积分由上下限与被积函数关系决定(因为定积分是一个确定的数值，而不定积分是一族函数，自变量不同，函数就不同).
:::

<Badge type="note">2.线性性质</Badge>

(1) $\begin{align*}\int_a^b [f(x) + g(x)] \mathrm{d}x = \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x + \int_a^b g(x) \mathrm{d}x\end{align*}$  
(2) $\begin{align*}\int_a^b k f(x) \mathrm{d}x = k \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x,k~为常数\end{align*}$

<Badge type="note">3.常数积分性质</Badge>

$\begin{align*}\int_a^b k \mathrm{d}x &= k(b - a),k~为常数 \\
\int_a^b 1 \mathrm{d}x &= b - a\end{align*}$

<Badge type="note">4.区间可加性</Badge>

$\begin{align*}\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = \int_a^c f(x) \mathrm{d}x + \int_c^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x\end{align*}$
::: warning 注意
不要求 $a < c < b,$只要积分存在就可以使用.
:::

<Badge type="note">5.比较定理</Badge>  

&emsp;&emsp;若在区间 $[a,b]$ 上恒有 $f(x) \ge g(x),$ 则有 
$$\begin{align*}\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x \ge \int_a^b g(x) \mathrm{d}x;\end{align*}$$
&emsp;&emsp;若 $f(x),|f(x)|$ 在 $[a,b]$ 上可积，则有 
$$\begin{align*}\Big|\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x\Big| \le \int_a^b |f(x)| \mathrm{d}x;\end{align*}$$

::: tip 特例
$\begin{align*}f(x) \ge 0,x \in [a,b] \Rightarrow \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x \ge 0\end{align*}$

:::

<Badge type="note">6.估值定理</Badge>

&emsp;&emsp;设 $M,m$ 为函数 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[a,b]$ 上的最大值和最小值，则有
$$\begin{align*}
m(b-a)\le\int_a^b f(x)\mathrm{d}x\le M(b-a);
\end{align*}$$

::: whiteboard
## 积分中值定理
<Badge type="note">7.积分中值定理</Badge>

&emsp;&emsp;设函数 $f(x)$ 在区间 $[a,b]$ 上连续，则至少存在一点 $\zeta \in [a,b],$ 使得
$$
\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = f(\zeta) (b - a).
$$  

<Badge type="note">证:</Badge>

$\begin{array}{l}
f(x)\in c[a,b]\Rightarrow \exists~m，M.使得 \\
\begin{aligned}
m \le& \quad\;\;\; f(x) \quad\le M& \\
\int_a^b m \mathrm{d}x \le& \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x \le \int_a^b M \mathrm{d}x \\
m(b - a) \le& \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x \le M(b - a) \\
m \le& \frac{\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x}{b - a} \le M \\
\end{aligned} \\
\begin{aligned}\exists~\zeta \in [a,b],使得~f(\zeta) = \frac{\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x}{b - a} \end{aligned}~(介值定理) \\
\begin{aligned}\Rightarrow \int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x = f(\zeta) (b - a),\zeta \in [a,b] \end{aligned}
\end{array}$
:::

## 特殊性质
1. $f(x) \in c[-a,a],$ 则
$$
\int_{-a}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^a [f(x)+f(-x)] \mathrm{d}x
$$

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge>$\begin{align*}f~奇函数:\int_{-a}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x = 0;\end{align*}$
<br>

<Badge type="warning">If</Badge>$\begin{align*}f~偶函数:\int_{-a}^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x = 2 \int_0^a f(x) \mathrm{d}x.\end{align*}$
<br>
<br>
<Badge type="danger">Notes:</Badge>

① $\begin{align*}\int_{-a}^0 f(x) \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x=-t} \int_0^a f(-x)\mathrm{d}x\end{align*}$  
② $\begin{align*}\int_a^{a+b} f(x) \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x=t+a} \int_0^b f(x+a)\mathrm{d}x\end{align*}$  
③ $\begin{align*}\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x+t=a+b} \int_a^b f(a+b-x)\mathrm{d}x\end{align*}$  
④ $\begin{align*}\int_a^b f(x) \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{x=a+(b-a)t} (b-a)\int_0^1 f(a+(b-a)x)\mathrm{d}x\end{align*}$


2. 设 $f(x) = c[0,1].$ 则  
(1) 
$$
\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\cos x) \mathrm{d}x
$$

> 根据 1.③ 即可得证

::: danger ⭐特别地
$\begin{array}{l}
\begin{align*}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^n x \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^n x \mathrm{d}x \xlongequal{\triangle} I_n(华莱士公式)\end{align*} \\
\left\{\begin{split} 
  I_n &= \frac{n-1}{n}I_{n-2} \\  
  I_0 &= \frac{\pi}{2},I_1 = 1.
\end{split}\right. 
\end{array}$

:::

(2)  
$$\begin{align*}
\int_0^\pi f(\sin x) \mathrm{d}x &= 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) \mathrm{d}x \\
&\Updownarrow \\
\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\sin x) \mathrm{d}x &= \int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^\pi f(\sin x) \mathrm{d}x
\end{align*}$$

> 根据 1.② 和 2.(1) 即可得证

$$\begin{align*}
\int_0^\pi f(|\cos x|) \mathrm{d}x &= 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\cos x) \mathrm{d}x \\
\int_0^\pi f(\cos^{2n} x) \mathrm{d}x &= 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} f(\cos^{2n} x) \mathrm{d}x
\end{align*}$$

(3)  
$$
\int_0^\pi xf(\sin x) \mathrm{d}x = \frac{\pi}{2}\int_0^\pi f(\sin x) \mathrm{d}x
$$

> 根据 1.③ 即可得证

::: faq-math \begin{align*}例1.~ I=\int_0^1 \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x+\sqrt{1-x^2}}\end{align*}#
$\begin{align*}
I &= \int_0^1 \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x+\sqrt{1-x^2}} \xlongequal{令x=\sin t} \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos t}{\sin t + \cos t}\mathrm{d}t = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos x}{\sin x + \cos x}\mathrm{d}x \\
I &= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos x}{\sin x + \cos x}\mathrm{d}x = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos x}{\sqrt{1 - \cos^2 x} + \cos x}\mathrm{d}x = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin x}{\sqrt{1 - \sin^2 x} + \sin x}\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin x}{\cos x + \sin x}\mathrm{d}x \\
2I &= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\cos x}{\sin x + \cos x}\mathrm{d}x + \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{\sin x}{\cos x + \sin x}\mathrm{d}x = \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} 1\cdot\mathrm{d}x = \frac{\pi}{2} \\
I &= \frac{\pi}{4}
\end{align*}$

:::

::: faq-math \begin{align*}例2.~ I=\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} \frac{\sin^2 x}{1+e^{-x}} \mathrm{d}x.\end{align*}#
$\begin{align*}
I &= \int_0^\pi \Big(\frac{\sin^2 x}{1+e^{-x}}+\frac{\sin^2 x}{1+e^x}\Big) \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int_0^\pi \Big(\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}}+\frac{1}{1+e^x}\Big)\sin^2 x \mathrm{d}x \\
&= \int_0^\pi \sin^2 x \mathrm{d}x \\
&= 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin^2 x \mathrm{d}x = 2\cdot I_2 = 2\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2} \\
&= \frac{\pi}{2}
\end{align*}$

:::


3. 设 $f(x)$ 连续且以 $T > 0$ 为周期，a 为常数，则
$$\begin{align*}
\int_a^{a+T} f(x) \mathrm{d}x &= \int_0^T f(x) \mathrm{d}x \\
\int_0^{nT} f(x) \mathrm{d}x &= n\int_0^T f(x) \mathrm{d}x~(n \in \mathbf{N})
\end{align*}
\tag{平移性质}$$

::: details: 证明
$\begin{align*}
\int_a^{a+T} f(x) \mathrm{d}x &= \int_a^0 f(x)\mathrm{d}x + \int_0^T f(x)\mathrm{d}x + \int_T^{a+T} f(x)\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \cancel{\int_a^0 f(x)\mathrm{d}x} + \int_0^T f(x)\mathrm{d}x + \cancel{\int_0^a f(x+T)\mathrm{d}x} \\
&= \int_0^T f(x)\mathrm{d}x
\end{align*}$  
$\begin{align*}
\int_0^{nT} f(x) \mathrm{d}x &= \int_0^T f(x)\mathrm{d}x + \int_T^{2T} f(x)\mathrm{d}x + \cdots +\int_{(n-1)T}^{nT} f(x)\mathrm{d}x \\
&= \sum_{k=1}^{n} \int_{(k-1)T}^{(k-1)T+T} f(x)\mathrm{d}x  = \sum_{k=1}^{n} \int_0^T f(x)\mathrm{d}x \\
&= n\int_0^T f(x)\mathrm{d}x
\end{align*}$

:::
